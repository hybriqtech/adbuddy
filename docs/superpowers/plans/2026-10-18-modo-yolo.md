# Modo YOLO: plan de implementación

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** que la persona usuaria pueda activar desde el móvil, durante 15 min, 1 h o hasta el fin de la sesión, un modo en el que el agente opera sin confirmaciones ni bloqueos fijos (salvo la pausa, que frena y apaga el modo), con aviso permanente en el móvil y rastro en el diario.

**Architecture:** el estado vive en el daemon (`daemon.Yolo`, solo en memoria, con reloj y temporizador inyectables) y cada puerta de `Daemon.exec`, de los ayudantes (`ui.go`) y de `undo` pregunta `d.lift(ctx, motivo)`: con YOLO activo la puerta se salta y el motivo se apunta para decírselo al agente por stderr (`⚠ YOLO: …`); sin YOLO todo queda como hoy. La app activa y apaga el modo con `yolo.set`/`yolo.state` (patrón de `remote.set`/`remote.state`); el IPC del shim y la CLI solo saben apagar y consultar. Las tarjetas de permiso de Claude Code se responden `allow` en el host. La app pinta una banda fija con cuenta atrás y una notificación persistente con «Apagar», y deja de proteger las apps protegidas del usuario mientras dura (ADBuddy sigue protegida: ruling R1). Activarlo exige además biometría fuerte o, si el móvil no la tiene, el bloqueo de pantalla (rulings R20 y R22).

**Tech Stack:** Go 1.27 (`host/`), Kotlin + Compose + kotlinx.serialization (`android/`), fixtures JSON compartidos (`protocol/fixtures/`), emulador API 34 para `emutest`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-adbuddy-modo-yolo-design.md` (complementa `2026-09-29-adbuddy-v1-design.md` §4 y `2026-10-01-adbuddy-protocolo-m3-m5-design.md` §8). Rulings del plan: `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-18-modo-yolo/progress.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Código y comentarios en inglés; docs, ledger y mensajes de commit en español como el resto del repo. Las cadenas dirigidas al agente (`⚠ YOLO: …`) son inglés fijo; las que lee la persona van al catálogo (`host/internal/i18n/catalog/{en,es}.toml`, `android/app/src/main/res/values{,-es}/strings.xml`) en el mismo commit.
- **Alcance decidido por la persona usuaria (no se reabre):** absolutamente todo salvo la pausa; la pausa frena y apaga YOLO; activación solo desde el móvil; duraciones 15 min, 1 h o `session`; no sobrevive a reiniciar el host; `rules.yaml` y el clasificador no cambian.
- **El agente no puede activar YOLO:** ni el IPC del shim (`exec`, `ui`, `undo`, `ping`, `hook`, `send`, `attach`, `session`, `remote`) ni la CLI tienen operación de activar; la CLI solo tiene `adbuddy yolo off|status`. Quien llega por `Handle(KindYolo)` con cualquier `Argv` que no sea `off`/`status` recibe `YOLO_ONLY_FROM_THE_PHONE`.
- **Activar exige autenticarse con el móvil (decidido por la persona usuaria):** el último paso de la activación en la app es `BiometricPrompt` con `BIOMETRIC_STRONG` solo si el móvil tiene biometría fuerte y, si no, la credencial del bloqueo de pantalla (`KeyguardManager.createConfirmDeviceCredentialIntent`: PIN, patrón o contraseña, con el riesgo de que un script del agente observe el PIN, apuntado en R22 y en `docs/safety.md`); sin bloqueo seguro, YOLO se activa igualmente tras un aviso específico y el retardo de armado (R20: el riesgo del toque inyectado lo acepta la persona usuaria, está en un ruling y en `docs/safety.md`). Dependencia nueva `androidx.biometric`. Un toque inyectado no la supera: con YOLO ya activo el agente puede dejar un proceso en segundo plano que abra ADBuddy y pulse «hasta el fin de la sesión» después de la caducidad, y la guarda del host no ve lo que hay dentro de un script.
- **La pregunta de `serve` mismo (`adbuddy remote on`, `AskUser`) es de la persona:** YOLO no levanta las puertas mientras espera (`liftQuestion`), o el agente podría pulsar «Permitir» en el móvil.
- **Lo que protege a ADBuddy no se levanta:** ADBuddy delante o en una mitad de pantalla dividida, lo que la propia app marca como protegido (`UIForeground.Protected`), la regla de «un comando que toca o lee la pantalla corre solo» y el «no sé qué hay delante». La app, con YOLO y el enlace arriba, es la que deja de marcar las apps protegidas del usuario.
- **Sin YOLO nada cambia:** cada prueba de una fila de la tabla de la spec se corre también sin YOLO (conducta actual) en el mismo test.
- La clasificación se sigue haciendo igual (sirve para el diario y el aviso). El diario se escribe siempre que se pueda; cada entrada lleva `yolo: true` si YOLO estaba activo al empezar.
- Las tarjetas de permiso de Claude Code se responden `allow`, **nunca** `allow_always`.
- Pausa: `d.paused()` es la única forma de preguntar por la pausa en el daemon (apaga YOLO al verla); ningún `d.o.Paused()` suelto.
- Protocolo: cambios **aditivos** (tipos `yolo.set`/`yolo.state`, campo `snapshot.yolo`, `permission.resolved.summary` y `by: "yolo"`, `journal.entry.yolo`), todos en la tarea T1 en un solo commit con Go, Kotlin y fixtures (protocolo §10). Necesita el visto bueno del líder (M5 §12).

`host/internal/daemon/yolo_test.go`:

```go
package daemon

import (
	"slices"
	"strings"
	"sync"
	"testing"
	"time"

	"github.com/hybriqtech/adbuddy/host/internal/journal"
)

// yoloClock is the clock and the timers of a Yolo under test: time only moves
// when the test says so.
type yoloClock struct {
	mu     sync.Mutex
	t      time.Time
	timers []*yoloTimer
	// lateStop makes the stop function a no-op: the Stop of a real AfterFunc can lose the race with the timer
	// firing, so a replaced timer may still run its function. Then only the generation check (and the clock)
	// protects the new state.
	lateStop bool
}

type yoloTimer struct {
	at      time.Time
	f       func()
	stopped bool
	fired   bool
}

func newYoloClock() *yoloClock {
	return &yoloClock{t: time.Date(2026, 10, 18, 12, 0, 0, 0, time.UTC)}
}

func (c *yoloClock) now() time.Time {
	c.mu.Lock()
	defer c.mu.Unlock()
	return c.t
}

func (c *yoloClock) after(d time.Duration, f func()) func() {
	c.mu.Lock()
	defer c.mu.Unlock()
	t := &yoloTimer{at: c.t.Add(d), f: f}
	c.timers = append(c.timers, t)
	return func() {
		c.mu.Lock()
		defer c.mu.Unlock()
		if !c.lateStop {
			t.stopped = true
		}
	}
}

// advance moves the clock and runs, oldest first, the timers that are due.
func (c *yoloClock) advance(d time.Duration) {
	c.jump(d)
	c.fireDue()
}

// jump moves the clock without running any timer: the PC slept through them.
func (c *yoloClock) jump(d time.Duration) {
	c.mu.Lock()
	defer c.mu.Unlock()
	c.t = c.t.Add(d)
}

func (c *yoloClock) fireDue() {
	for {
		c.mu.Lock()
		var next *yoloTimer
		for _, t := range c.timers {
			if !t.stopped && !t.fired && !t.at.After(c.t) && (next == nil || t.at.Before(next.at)) {
				next = t
			}
		}
		if next != nil {
			next.fired = true
		}
		c.mu.Unlock()
		if next == nil {
			return
		}
		next.f()
	}
}

// yoloSeen records what the observers are told.
```

```go
package daemon

import (
	"errors"
	"slices"
	"sync"
	"time"

	"github.com/hybriqtech/adbuddy/host/internal/classify"
	"github.com/hybriqtech/adbuddy/host/internal/i18n"
	"github.com/hybriqtech/adbuddy/host/internal/journal"
)

// YOLO mode (spec 2026-10-18-adbuddy-modo-yolo-design.md): for a time the
// person chose on the phone, the agent works directly. Only the phone turns it
// on (the app's authenticated channel; nothing the agent can reach), and it
// lives in memory: it does not survive serve. The pause, `adbuddy yolo off`,
// the app and the expiry turn it off.

// The durations Enable accepts.
const (
	YoloShort = 15 * time.Minute
	YoloLong  = time.Hour
)

// ErrYoloDuration is Enable's error for any other duration.
var ErrYoloDuration = errors.New("YOLO lasts 15 minutes, 1 hour or until the session ends")

// YoloWhy says what changed YOLO.
type YoloWhy string

const (
	YoloOn         YoloWhy = "on"
	YoloOffApp     YoloWhy = "app"     // yolo.set off from the phone
	YoloOffCLI     YoloWhy = "cli"     // adbuddy yolo off
	YoloOffExpired YoloWhy = "expired" // its time is up
	YoloOffPause   YoloWhy = "pause"   // the agent was paused from the phone
)

// YoloState is YOLO now.
type YoloState struct {
	Active  bool
	Until   time.Time // zero when off or until the session ends
	Session bool      // until serve stops
}

// YoloChange is what an observer is told: the state after the change and why.
type YoloChange struct {
	State YoloState
	Why   YoloWhy
}

// Yolo is the state of the mode. It is safe for concurrent use. now and after
// are the clock and the timer, injectable for the tests.
//
// Lock order: notifyMu, then mu. A change and the calls to its observers
// happen under notifyMu, so the observers see the changes in order; they run
// without mu, and may call State and Active but never Enable, EnableSession
// or Off.
type Yolo struct {
	now   func() time.Time
	after func(d time.Duration, f func()) (stop func())

	notifyMu  sync.Mutex
	mu        sync.Mutex
	until     time.Time
	session   bool
	gen       uint64 // a timer only acts for the generation it was armed in
	stop      func()
	late      bool // Active found the expiry passed and has scheduled telling it: once
	observers []func(YoloChange)
}

// NewYolo returns YOLO, off. nil now is time.Now and nil after is time.AfterFunc.
func NewYolo(now func() time.Time, after func(d time.Duration, f func()) (stop func())) *Yolo {
	if now == nil {
		now = time.Now
	}
	if after == nil {
		after = func(d time.Duration, f func()) func() {
			t := time.AfterFunc(d, f)
			return func() { t.Stop() }
		}
	}
	return &Yolo{now: now, after: after}
}

// Subscribe registers f to be told of every change (including the expiry).
func (y *Yolo) Subscribe(f func(YoloChange)) {
	y.mu.Lock()
	defer y.mu.Unlock()
	y.observers = append(y.observers, f)
}

// State is YOLO now. An expiry that has passed counts as off even if its
// timer has not run yet.
func (y *Yolo) State() YoloState {
	y.mu.Lock()
	defer y.mu.Unlock()
	return y.stateLocked()
}

func (y *Yolo) stateLocked() YoloState {
	switch {
	case y.session:
		return YoloState{Active: true, Session: true}
	case !y.until.IsZero() && y.now().Before(y.until):
		return YoloState{Active: true, Until: y.until}
	}
	return YoloState{}
}

// Active reports whether YOLO is on. It asks the clock, not the timer: if the
// expiry has passed and nobody has been told (the PC slept), the telling is
// scheduled.
func (y *Yolo) Active() bool {
	y.mu.Lock()
	defer y.mu.Unlock()
	if y.session {
		return true
	}
	if y.until.IsZero() {
		return false
	}
	if y.now().Before(y.until) {
		return true
	}
	if !y.late {
		y.late = true
		g := y.gen
		y.after(0, func() { y.expire(g) })
	}
	return false
}

// Enable turns YOLO on for d, which must be YoloShort or YoloLong. Turning it
// on again replaces the time.
func (y *Yolo) Enable(d time.Duration) error {
	if d != YoloShort && d != YoloLong {
		return ErrYoloDuration
	}
	y.change(func() (YoloChange, bool) {
		y.disarmLocked()
		y.until = y.now().Add(d).Round(0) // wall clock only: the PC may sleep
		g := y.gen
		y.stop = y.after(d, func() { y.expire(g) })
		return YoloChange{State: y.stateLocked(), Why: YoloOn}, true
	})
	return nil
}

// EnableSession turns YOLO on until serve stops.
func (y *Yolo) EnableSession() {
	y.change(func() (YoloChange, bool) {
		y.disarmLocked()
		y.session = true
		return YoloChange{State: y.stateLocked(), Why: YoloOn}, true
	})
}

// Off turns YOLO off, for why. Off while off tells nobody; off over an expiry
// nobody has been told yet tells that expiry.
func (y *Yolo) Off(why YoloWhy) {
	y.change(func() (YoloChange, bool) {
		set := y.session || !y.until.IsZero()
		was := y.stateLocked().Active
		y.disarmLocked()
		switch {
		case was:
			return YoloChange{Why: why}, true
		case set:
			return YoloChange{Why: YoloOffExpired}, true
		}
		return YoloChange{}, false
	})
}

// expire is the timer's: it switches YOLO off if g is still the generation it
// was armed for and the expiry has come.
func (y *Yolo) expire(g uint64) {
	y.change(func() (YoloChange, bool) {
		if g != y.gen || y.session || y.until.IsZero() {
			return YoloChange{}, false
		}
		if now := y.now(); now.Before(y.until) { // it ran early (the wall clock moved): wait for the rest
			y.stop = y.after(y.until.Sub(now), func() { y.expire(g) })
			return YoloChange{}, false
		}
		y.disarmLocked()
		return YoloChange{Why: YoloOffExpired}, true
	})
}

// disarmLocked clears the state and the timer. mu must be held.
func (y *Yolo) disarmLocked() {
	if y.stop != nil {
		y.stop()
		y.stop = nil
	}
	y.gen++
	y.until, y.session, y.late = time.Time{}, false, false
}

// change runs f under both locks and tells the observers if it changed
// something.
func (y *Yolo) change(f func() (YoloChange, bool)) {
	y.notifyMu.Lock()
	defer y.notifyMu.Unlock()
	y.mu.Lock()
	c, ok := f()
	obs := slices.Clone(y.observers)
	y.mu.Unlock()
	if !ok {
		return
	}
	for _, o := range obs {
		o(c)
	}
}

// YoloText is what the person reads when YOLO changes, in lang: the journal's
// description and serve's log line.
func YoloText(lang i18n.Lang, c YoloChange) string {
	switch c.Why {
	case YoloOn:
		if c.State.Session {
			return lang.T("yolo.event.on_session")
		}
		return lang.T("yolo.event.on_until", c.State.Until.Local().Format("15:04"))
	case YoloOffApp:
		return lang.T("yolo.event.off_app")
	case YoloOffCLI:
		return lang.T("yolo.event.off_cli")
	case YoloOffPause:
		return lang.T("yolo.event.off_pause")
	}
	return lang.T("yolo.event.off_expired")
}

// yoloEvent journals a change of YOLO. It is an event, not a change of the
// phone: green, nothing to undo.
func (d *Daemon) yoloEvent(c YoloChange) {
	now := d.o.Now().UTC()
	rule, code := "yolo.off", 0
	if c.Why == YoloOn {
		rule = "yolo.on"
	}
	d.record(journal.Entry{
		ID: journal.NewID("j_", now), TS: now, Session: d.o.Session, Serial: d.identity(), Source: journal.SourceUser,
		Argv: []string{"yolo", string(c.Why)}, Level: classify.Green.String(), Rule: rule,
		Description: YoloText(d.lang(), c), Status: journal.StatusOK, ExitCode: &code,
	})
}
```

```go
// yoloOn turns YOLO on until the end of the test's session.
func (e *env) yoloOn(t *testing.T) {
	t.Helper()
	e.d.Yolo().EnableSession()
}

// yoloRun is one command run in its own environment.
type yoloRun struct {
	e    *env
	s    *stream
	exit ipc.Exit
}

// yoloPair runs argv twice, each in a fresh environment prepared by setup:
// without YOLO (the behaviour of today) and with it.
func yoloPair(t *testing.T, setup func(*env), argv ...string) (off, on yoloRun) {
	t.Helper()
	for _, yolo := range []bool{false, true} {
		e := newEnv(t)
		if setup != nil {
			setup(e)
		}
		if yolo {
			e.yoloOn(t)
		}
		s, exit := e.exec(t, argv...)
		if yolo {
			on = yoloRun{e, s, exit}
		} else {
			off = yoloRun{e, s, exit}
		}
	}
	return off, on
}

func (r yoloRun) ran(sub string) bool { return r.e.ranContains(sub) }

// warned reports whether the agent was told, on stderr, what YOLO let through.
func (r yoloRun) warned(sub string) bool {
	out := r.s.errOut.String()
	return strings.Contains(out, "⚠ YOLO: ") && strings.Contains(out, sub)
}

func (r yoloRun) stderr() string { return r.s.errOut.String() }

	}
```

```go
package daemon

import (
	"context"
	"fmt"
	"io"
	"sync"

	"github.com/hybriqtech/adbuddy/host/internal/classify"
)

// noRecord is what the agent is told when the journal could not be written
// and the command ran anyway.
const noRecord = "the journal cannot be written: this ran without a record and cannot be undone"

// yoloNotes collects, for one request, what YOLO let through that would have
// been asked or refused. They are told to the agent on stderr right before the
// command runs (`⚠ YOLO: …`), so it knows what the person did not get to see.
type yoloNotes struct {
	mu    sync.Mutex
	lines []string
}

type yoloNotesKey struct{}

// withYoloNotes returns ctx with a collector for one request.
func withYoloNotes(ctx context.Context) (context.Context, *yoloNotes) {
	n := &yoloNotes{}
	return context.WithValue(ctx, yoloNotesKey{}, n), n
}

func (n *yoloNotes) add(why string) {
	n.mu.Lock()
	defer n.mu.Unlock()
	for _, l := range n.lines {
		if l == why {
			return
		}
	}
	n.lines = append(n.lines, why)
}

// tell writes the notes, one per line.
func (n *yoloNotes) tell(w io.Writer) {
	n.mu.Lock()
	defer n.mu.Unlock()
	for _, l := range n.lines {
		fmt.Fprintln(w, "⚠ YOLO: "+l)
	}
}

// lift reports whether YOLO lets the request pass a door that would have
// refused or asked, for the reason why (English, for the agent), and notes it.
// Without YOLO it is false and nothing is noted. It is asked at the door, not
// once at the start: an off from the phone takes effect at the next door.
func (d *Daemon) lift(ctx context.Context, why string) bool {
	if !d.o.Yolo.Active() {
		return false
	}
	if n, _ := ctx.Value(yoloNotesKey{}).(*yoloNotes); n != nil {
		n.add(why)
	}
	return true
}

// liftQuestion is lift for the doors that a question waiting for an answer
// closes (waitingFor, admit): text is what they say (blocks). It is false for
// a question asked through AskUser (serve's own `adbuddy remote on`, and the 🔴 inverse of an undo), which is the
// person's, not the agent's command's: while it waits, an `input tap` could
// press Allow on the phone and approve it for them. YOLO lifts the doors for
// the agent's own 🔴 questions only.
func (d *Daemon) liftQuestion(ctx context.Context, text string) bool {
	if d.waitingOnOwn() {
		return false
	}
	return d.lift(ctx, "a question was waiting for your answer ("+classify.Printable(text)+")")
}

// waitingOnOwn reports whether the question waiting is serve's own (AskUser).
func (d *Daemon) waitingOnOwn() bool {
	d.askMu.Lock()
	defer d.askMu.Unlock()
	return d.asking != nil && d.asking.own
}

// flyAnyway counts a change in flight even if a question is waiting: YOLO
// runs the command anyway, and the question (if one is ever asked) waits for
// it to end, as for any change.
func (d *Daemon) flyAnyway(change bool, title string) (release func()) {
	if !change {
		return func() {}
	}
	d.askMu.Lock()
	defer d.askMu.Unlock()
	return d.flyLocked(title)
}
```

```go
package daemon

import "github.com/hybriqtech/adbuddy/host/internal/ipc"

// yoloCmd answers `adbuddy yolo off|status`. It has no "on": the IPC is what
// the agent reaches (the shim and the CLI), and a prompt injection must not be
// able to take its own brakes off. Only the phone app turns YOLO on, by the
// bridge's authenticated channel (cli/phone_yolo.go).
func (d *Daemon) yoloCmd(req ipc.Request, s Stream) ipc.Exit {
	if len(req.Argv) != 1 {
		return ipc.Exit{Code: 2, ErrorCode: "YOLO_INVALID", Error: "use yolo off or yolo status"}
	}
	switch req.Argv[0] {
	case "off":
		d.o.Yolo.Off(YoloOffCLI)
	case "status":
	default:
		return ipc.Exit{Code: ExitDenied, ErrorCode: "YOLO_ONLY_FROM_THE_PHONE", Error: "YOLO can only be turned on from the phone app"}
	}
	st := d.o.Yolo.State()
	out := ipc.YoloStatus{Active: st.Active, Session: st.Session}
	if st.Active && !st.Session {
		out.Until = st.Until.UnixMilli()
	}
	_ = s.Data(out)
	return ipc.Exit{}
}
```

```go
// yoloInfo is the YOLO mode as `adbuddy yolo` reports it. Serve says whether
// `adbuddy serve` answered: YOLO lives in its memory, so with it stopped the
// mode is off.
type yoloInfo struct {
	Active  bool       `json:"active"`
	Session bool       `json:"session,omitempty"`
	Until   *time.Time `json:"until,omitempty"`
	Serve   bool       `json:"serve"`
}

// newYoloCmd is `adbuddy yolo off|status`. There is no `on`: only the phone
// app turns the mode on, and the agent may run this command (turning it off is
// always safe).
func newYoloCmd(app *App) *cobra.Command {
	return &cobra.Command{
		Use:       "yolo off|status",
		Short:     app.T("help.yolo.short"),
		Long:      app.T("help.yolo.long"),
		Example:   "  adbuddy yolo status\n  adbuddy yolo off",
		Args:      cobra.ArbitraryArgs,
		ValidArgs: []string{"off", "status"},
		RunE: func(cmd *cobra.Command, args []string) error {
			if len(args) != 1 || args[0] != "off" && args[0] != "status" {
				return UsageErrorT("err.yolocmd.usage")
			}
			op := args[0]
			e, err := loadEnv(app)
			if err != nil {
				return err
			}
			var wire ipc.YoloStatus
			exit, err := ipc.Call(cmd.Context(), e.dirs.Data, ipc.Request{Kind: ipc.KindYolo, Argv: []string{op}},
				nil, app.Stderr, app.Stderr, func(b []byte) error { return json.Unmarshal(b, &wire) })
			info := yoloInfo{Serve: true}
			switch {
			case errors.Is(err, ipc.ErrNotRunning):
				info = yoloInfo{}
			case err != nil:
				if cmd.Context().Err() != nil {
					return Interrupted()
				}
				return NewError("DAEMON_UNREACHABLE", err.Error(), "")
			case exit.Code != 0:
				return NewError(orDefault(exit.ErrorCode, "DAEMON_ERROR"), exit.Error, "")
			default:
				info.Active, info.Session = wire.Active, wire.Session
				if wire.Active && !wire.Session {
					t := time.UnixMilli(wire.Until).UTC()
					info.Until = &t
				}
			}
			if app.JSON {
				return WriteData(app.Stdout, info)
			}
			fmt.Fprintln(app.Stdout, yoloTextIn(app.speaks(), info))
			return nil
		},
	}
}

	case !i.Serve:

	case !i.Active:
		return lang.T("yolocmd.off")
	case i.Session:
		return lang.T("yolocmd.on_session")
	}
	return lang.T("yolocmd.on_until", i.Until.Local().Format("15:04"))
}
```

```go
// YOLO mode's part of the phone link (spec 2026-10-18-adbuddy-modo-yolo-design.md):
// yolo.set from the app and yolo.state to it. The state lives in daemon.Yolo;
// the daemon is created after the link, so serve gives it with useYolo.
//
// This is the only place that turns YOLO on, and only for a yolo.set that came
// by the app's authenticated channel (TestYoloIsOnlyTurnedOnByThePhoneLink).

// useYolo gives the link the daemon's YOLO state and makes the link tell the
// person (serve's log) and the app of every change.
func (l *phoneLink) useYolo(y *daemon.Yolo) {
	l.mu.Lock()
	l.yolo = y
	l.mu.Unlock()
	y.Subscribe(l.yoloChanged)
}

// yoloOf is the YOLO state, nil until serve gives it.
func (l *phoneLink) yoloOf() *daemon.Yolo {
	l.mu.Lock()
	defer l.mu.Unlock()
	return l.yolo
}

// toBridgeYolo is the state as the app sees it.
func toBridgeYolo(s daemon.YoloState) bridge.YoloState {
	out := bridge.YoloState{Active: s.Active, Session: s.Active && s.Session}
	if s.Active && !s.Session {
		out.Until = s.Until.UnixMilli()
	}
	return out
}

// yoloChanged tells serve's log and the app that YOLO changed (an event). It
// runs under the observers' lock of daemon.Yolo: it never turns YOLO on or off. l.srv, l.log and l.lang are set
// by startPhoneLink before useYolo and never change after, so they are read without l.mu.
func (l *phoneLink) yoloChanged(c daemon.YoloChange) {
	if l.log != nil {
		fmt.Fprintln(l.log, "⚠ "+daemon.YoloText(l.lang, c))
	}
	if l.srv != nil {
		_ = l.srv.Publish(bridge.TypeYoloState, toBridgeYolo(c.State))
	}
}

// yoloArea is YOLO's part of the phone link (phone_areas.go).
func (l *phoneLink) yoloArea() area {
	return area{
		handlers: map[string]func(bridge.Message){bridge.TypeYoloSet: l.handleYoloSet},
		snapshot: func(s *bridge.Snapshot) {
			if y := l.yoloOf(); y != nil {
				s.Yolo = toBridgeYolo(y.State())
			}
		},
		// attach runs with the server's lock held: publishing waits for it.
		attach: func() {
			if y := l.yoloOf(); y != nil {
				go func() { _ = l.srv.Publish(bridge.TypeYoloState, toBridgeYolo(y.State())) }()
			}
		},
	}
}

// handleYoloSet turns YOLO on or off at the app's request. It comes from the
// current authenticated connection only (the bridge's token), and every
// request is answered: with yolo.state carrying its rid, or an error.
func (l *phoneLink) handleYoloSet(m bridge.Message) {
	srv := l.srv
	if srv == nil || !srv.Current(m) { // no app, or a connection already replaced
		return
	}
	if m.RID == "" {
		// A request by protocol: with no rid there is nobody to answer.
		fmt.Fprintln(l.log, l.lang.T("phone.yolo_set_without_rid"))
		return
	}
	fail := func(code, text string) {
		_ = srv.Reply(m, bridge.TypeError, bridge.ErrorReply{Code: code, Text: text})
	}
	y := l.yoloOf()
	if y == nil {
		fail(bridge.CodeUnavailable, "YOLO mode is not available on this host.")
		return
	}
	var req bridge.YoloSet
	if err := json.Unmarshal(m.Payload, &req); err != nil || !exactlyOne(req) {
		fail(bridge.CodeInvalid, `yolo.set needs {"minutes": 15 or 60}, {"session": true} or {"off": true}, only one of them.`)
		return
	}
	switch {
	case req.Off:
		y.Off(daemon.YoloOffApp)
	case l.Paused():
		// The pause would turn it off at the next command: say so instead of a mode that is off already.
		fail(bridge.CodeFailed, "The agent is paused: resume it before turning YOLO on.")
		return
	case req.Session:
		y.EnableSession()
	default:
		var d time.Duration
		switch req.Minutes {
		case 15:
			d = daemon.YoloShort
		case 60:
			d = daemon.YoloLong
		}
		if err := y.Enable(d); err != nil { // any other number
			fail(bridge.CodeInvalid, err.Error())
			return
		}
	}
	_ = srv.PublishRID(bridge.TypeYoloState, m.RID, toBridgeYolo(y.State()))
}

// exactlyOne reports whether req asks for one thing: a number of minutes, the
// session, or off.
func exactlyOne(req bridge.YoloSet) bool {
	n := 0
	if req.Minutes != 0 {
		n++
	}
	if req.Session {
		n++
	}
	if req.Off {
		n++
	}
	return n == 1
}
```

```kotlin
package tech.hybriq.adbuddy.bridge

import kotlinx.serialization.json.JsonElement
import kotlinx.serialization.json.JsonObject
import kotlinx.serialization.json.JsonPrimitive
import kotlinx.serialization.json.booleanOrNull

/**
 * The YOLO mode's events (protocol spec §8.3). Three values: on, off, or unknown (null). A payload this app cannot
 * read is "unknown", never "off": a false off would hide the band of a mode that is on.
 */
object YoloReducer : Reducer {
    override val types = setOf(Protocol.YOLO_STATE)

    override fun event(s: BridgeState, m: Message): BridgeState = s.copy(yolo = read(m.payload))

    // Per area (protocol §4.3): a malformed yolo part must not make the whole snapshot unreadable.
    override fun snapshot(s: BridgeState, snap: Snapshot): BridgeState = s.copy(yolo = snap.yolo?.let(::read))

    // active has a default (false) and the decoder coerces nulls, so {} would read as "off": it must be there as a
    // real boolean, or the state is unknown.
    private fun read(el: JsonElement): YoloState? {
        val active = (el as? JsonObject)?.get("active") as? JsonPrimitive
        if (active == null || active.isString || active.booleanOrNull == null) return null
        return try {
            Protocol.json.decodeFromJsonElement(YoloState.serializer(), el)
        } catch (e: IllegalArgumentException) {
            null
        } catch (e: StackOverflowError) {
            null
        }
    }

    override fun reset(s: BridgeState, why: Reset): BridgeState = when (why) {
        Reset.NEW_SESSION, Reset.UNREADABLE_SNAPSHOT -> s.copy(yolo = null)
        // The mode lives on the host and outlives the link (spec): what was known stays as it was.
        Reset.DISCONNECTED, Reset.INCOMPATIBLE -> s
    }
}
```

```kotlin
package tech.hybriq.adbuddy.yolo

import java.util.Locale
import kotlinx.serialization.json.JsonElement
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.bridge.BridgeState
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.bridge.Protocol
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.bridge.YoloSet
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.confirm.ConfirmUi

/**
 * What the YOLO mode's card, band, dialog and notification show and do (spec 2026-10-18). Pure, so it is
 * unit-tested. Times are in the host's clock (HostClock.now()), which `until` is written in.
 */
object YoloUi {
    /** UNKNOWN: the host has not said (or is too old to have the mode); never painted as off. */
    enum class Kind { UNKNOWN, OFF, TIMED, SESSION }

    data class View(val kind: Kind, val msLeft: Long = 0) {
        val active: Boolean get() = kind == Kind.TIMED || kind == Kind.SESSION
    }

    /**
     * An expiry that has passed while the mode still reads as active is 0:00, not off: only the host says it is off
     * (it tells at the expiry), and a false off would hide a mode that is on. The link being down changes nothing: the
     * mode lives on the host.
     */
    fun view(state: BridgeState, now: Long): View {
        val y = state.yolo ?: return View(Kind.UNKNOWN)
        return when {
            !y.active -> View(Kind.OFF)
            y.session -> View(Kind.SESSION)
            else -> View(Kind.TIMED, (y.until - now).coerceAtLeast(0))
        }
    }

    /** m:ss or h:mm:ss, rounded up to whole seconds (so 0:00 only means the time is up). */
    fun countdown(msLeft: Long): String {
        val s = (msLeft.coerceAtLeast(0) + 999) / 1000
        val h = s / 3600
        val m = s % 3600 / 60
        return if (h > 0) String.format(Locale.ROOT, "%d:%02d:%02d", h, m, s % 60) else String.format(Locale.ROOT, "%d:%02d", m, s % 60)
    }

    /** The three durations the host accepts. */
    enum class Choice(val minutes: Int, val session: Boolean) { QUARTER(15, false), HOUR(60, false), SESSION(0, true) }

    fun request(choice: Choice): JsonElement =
        Protocol.json.encodeToJsonElement(YoloSet.serializer(), YoloSet(minutes = choice.minutes, session = choice.session))

    fun offRequest(): JsonElement = Protocol.json.encodeToJsonElement(YoloSet.serializer(), YoloSet(off = true))

    /** The switch turns the mode on only from a known off with the PC connected. Turning it off is never limited. */
    fun canTurnOn(view: View, connected: Boolean): Boolean = view.kind == Kind.OFF && connected

    /**
     * Whether the button of the activation dialog may send: a duration was chosen (there is no default), the PC is
     * connected, the phone is unlocked now (read again at the tap: the dialog could have been left open) and the button
     * has been shown for ConfirmUi.ARMING_MS ([shownAt] and [now] in the phone's elapsed time), so a stray tap or a
     * burst as the dialog pops up turns nothing on.
     */
    fun mayConfirm(choice: Choice?, shownAt: Long?, now: Long, locked: Boolean, connected: Boolean): Boolean =
        choice != null && connected && !locked && ConfirmUi.armed(shownAt, now)
}
```

```kotlin
/** The YOLO card of Settings and the activation dialog, in every ScreenMode. */
@RunWith(ParameterizedRobolectricTestRunner::class)
@GraphicsMode(GraphicsMode.Mode.NATIVE)
@Config(qualifiers = PHONE)
class YoloScreensTest(private val mode: ScreenMode) {
    private val compose = createComposeRule()

    @get:Rule val rules: RuleChain = RuleChain.outerRule(ScreenModeRule(mode)).around(ComponentActivityRule()).around(compose)

    private val app get() = RuntimeEnvironment.getApplication() as AdbuddyApp
    private fun text(id: Int, vararg args: Any): String = app.getString(id, *args)
    private fun connected(y: YoloState?) = BridgeState(connection = BridgeState.Connection.CONNECTED, yolo = y)

    private var turnedOn = 0
    private var turnedOff = 0

    private fun card(state: BridgeState) {
        compose.setContent {
            AdbuddyTheme(dynamic = false) { YoloCard(state, app.clock, onTurnOn = { turnedOn++ }, onTurnOff = { turnedOff++ }) }
        }
    }

    @Test fun offUnknownOnAndDisconnected() {
        card(connected(YoloState()))
        compose.onNodeWithText(text(R.string.yolo_title)).assertIsDisplayed()
        compose.onNodeWithText(text(R.string.yolo_off)).assertIsDisplayed()
        compose.assertTextsFit()
        compose.shot("yolo_card_off", mode)
    }

    @Test fun unknownDrawsNoSwitchToTurnOn() {
        card(connected(null))
        compose.onNodeWithText(text(R.string.yolo_unknown)).assertIsDisplayed()
        compose.onNode(isToggleable()).assertIsNotEnabled()
    }

    @Test fun disconnectedCannotTurnOn() {
        card(BridgeState(yolo = YoloState()))
        compose.onNodeWithText(text(R.string.yolo_disconnected)).assertIsDisplayed()
        compose.onNode(isToggleable()).assertIsNotEnabled()
    }

    @Test fun onWithSessionAndTurningItOffAsksNothing() {
        card(connected(YoloState(active = true, session = true)))
        compose.onNodeWithText(text(R.string.yolo_on_session)).assertIsDisplayed()
        compose.assertTextsFit()
        compose.shot("yolo_card_session", mode)
        compose.onNode(isToggleable()).performClick()
        assertEquals("off is one tap, with no question", 1, turnedOff)
        assertEquals(0, turnedOn)
    }

    @Test fun turnedOffTheSwitchOnlyAsks() {
        card(connected(YoloState()))
        compose.onNode(isToggleable()).performClick()
        assertEquals(1, turnedOn)
        assertEquals(0, turnedOff)
    }

    // The dialog: its clock is the test's, so the arming wait is exact.
    private var elapsed = 10_000L
    private var locked = false
    private var confirmed: YoloUi.Choice? = null

    // The clock of Compose is the test's too (no autoAdvance): the arming wait ends when the test says so.
    private fun dialog(connected: Boolean = true) {
        compose.mainClock.autoAdvance = false
        compose.setContent {
            AdbuddyTheme(dynamic = false) {
                YoloActivationDialog(connected, isLocked = { locked }, onConfirm = { confirmed = it }, onDismiss = {}, elapsed = { elapsed })
            }
        }
        settle()
    }

    /** Lets the frames after an interaction run. */
    private fun settle() = compose.mainClock.advanceTimeBy(50)

    /**
     * The phone's time and Compose's go by ConfirmUi.ARMING_MS: the button has been shown long enough. Twice, because
     * the dialog arms again each time the activity resumes (LifecycleResumeEffect), which in some ScreenModes (a
     * configuration change by the font scale) happens after the first wait started.

    private fun arm() {
        repeat(2) {
            elapsed += ConfirmUi.ARMING_MS
            // One jump of Compose's clock, not 40 frames: at 200 % text each frame of the dialog costs memory.
            compose.mainClock.advanceTimeBy(ConfirmUi.ARMING_MS + 100, ignoreFrameDuration = true)
            settle()
        }
    }

        // scrolling to them with the clock stopped never ends (the test JVM runs out of memory).
        compose.onNodeWithText(text(id)).performSemanticsAction(SemanticsActions.OnClick)
        settle()
    }

            compose.onNodeWithText(text(id)).performScrollTo().assertIsDisplayed()

    @Test fun thereIsNoDefaultDurationAndTheButtonIsOffUntilOneIsChosen() {
        dialog()
        arm()
        compose.onNodeWithText(text(R.string.yolo_confirm)).assertIsNotEnabled() // armed, but nothing chosen
    }

    // Mutation (each): drop the choice or the arming from mayConfirm (YoloUi).
    @Test fun nothingIsSentWithoutAChoiceAndTheArmingWait() {

        compose.onNodeWithText(text(R.string.yolo_confirm)).assertIsEnabled().performClick()
```

```kotlin
const val YOLO_CARD_TAG = "yolo_card"

/** The YOLO view now: it recomposes every second while there is a countdown (HostClock is not observable). */
@Composable
fun rememberYoloView(state: BridgeState, clock: HostClock): YoloUi.View {
    var tick by remember { mutableIntStateOf(0) }
    val counting = state.yolo?.let { it.active && !it.session } == true
    LaunchedEffect(counting) {
        while (counting) {
            delay(1000)
            tick++
        }
    }
    return YoloUi.view(state, clock.now() + tick * 0L) // reads tick so the view recomposes
}

/**
 * The YOLO switch of Settings. The logic is YoloUi; this only draws it. Turning it on only asks (the dialog is the
 * caller's and is what sends anything); turning it off is one tap, with no question: off is always safe.
 */
@Composable
fun YoloCard(state: BridgeState, clock: HostClock, onTurnOn: () -> Unit, onTurnOff: () -> Unit, modifier: Modifier = Modifier) {
    val view = rememberYoloView(state, clock)
    val connected = state.connection == BridgeState.Connection.CONNECTED
    // Off stays available whatever the link says (the request may fail: the caller says so); on needs a known off and the PC.
    val enabled = view.active || YoloUi.canTurnOn(view, connected)
    ElevatedCard(modifier.fillMaxWidth().padding(horizontal = 12.dp, vertical = 4.dp).testTag(YOLO_CARD_TAG)) {
        Row(
            Modifier
                .toggleable(value = view.active, enabled = enabled, role = Role.Switch, onValueChange = { if (it) onTurnOn() else onTurnOff() })
                .padding(12.dp),
            horizontalArrangement = Arrangement.spacedBy(12.dp),
            verticalAlignment = Alignment.CenterVertically,
        ) {
            Column(Modifier.weight(1f), verticalArrangement = Arrangement.spacedBy(2.dp)) {
                Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_title), style = MaterialTheme.typography.titleMedium)
                val problem = view.active || view.kind == Kind.UNKNOWN || (!connected && !view.active)
                Text(
                    when {
                        view.kind == Kind.TIMED -> stringResource(R.string.yolo_on_left, YoloUi.countdown(view.msLeft))
                        view.kind == Kind.SESSION -> stringResource(R.string.yolo_on_session)
                        view.kind == Kind.UNKNOWN -> stringResource(R.string.yolo_unknown)
                        !connected -> stringResource(R.string.yolo_disconnected)
                        else -> stringResource(R.string.yolo_off)
                    },
                    style = MaterialTheme.typography.bodySmall,
                    color = if (problem) MaterialTheme.colorScheme.error else MaterialTheme.colorScheme.onSurfaceVariant,
                )
            }
            Switch(checked = view.active, onCheckedChange = null, enabled = enabled, modifier = Modifier.minimumInteractiveComponentSize())
        }
    }
}
```

```kotlin
private val YoloUi.Choice.label: Int
    get() = when (this) {
        YoloUi.Choice.QUARTER -> R.string.yolo_for_15
        YoloUi.Choice.HOUR -> R.string.yolo_for_60
        YoloUi.Choice.SESSION -> R.string.yolo_for_session
    }

/**
 * The warning that turns YOLO on (spec 2026-10-18, App Android). It lists what is skipped, makes the person choose a
 * duration (none is chosen for them), and its button sends nothing until the choice is made, the PC is connected, the
 * phone is unlocked (read again at the tap: [isLocked]) and the button has been shown for ConfirmUi.ARMING_MS
 * (counted again each time the app comes back to the front): the same discipline as Allow in a red question.
 * [elapsed] is the phone's elapsed time (a parameter for the tests).
 */
@Composable
fun YoloActivationDialog(
    connected: Boolean,
    isLocked: () -> Boolean,
    onConfirm: (YoloUi.Choice) -> Unit,
    onDismiss: () -> Unit,
    elapsed: () -> Long = SystemClock::elapsedRealtime,
) {
    var picked by rememberSaveable { mutableStateOf<String?>(null) }
    val choice = picked?.let { name -> YoloUi.Choice.entries.firstOrNull { it.name == name } }
    var shownAt by remember { mutableLongStateOf(elapsed()) }
    var tick by remember { mutableIntStateOf(0) }
    LifecycleResumeEffect(Unit) {
        shownAt = elapsed()
        onPauseOrDispose {}
    }
    // A tick when the wait is over, so the button is drawn enabled.
    LaunchedEffect(shownAt) {
        delay(ConfirmUi.ARMING_MS + 50)
        tick++
    }
    val armed = YoloUi.mayConfirm(choice, shownAt, elapsed() + tick * 0L, locked = false, connected = connected)
    AlertDialog(
        onDismissRequest = onDismiss,
        title = { Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_dialog_title)) },
        text = {
            ObscuredTouchGuard()
            Column(Modifier.verticalScroll(rememberScrollState()), verticalArrangement = Arrangement.spacedBy(8.dp)) {
                Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_dialog_intro))
                for (id in listOf(R.string.yolo_skip_confirmations, R.string.yolo_skip_blocks, R.string.yolo_skip_journal, R.string.yolo_skip_protected, R.string.yolo_skip_permissions)) {
                    Text(stringResource(id), style = MaterialTheme.typography.bodyMedium)
                }
                Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_pause_note), style = MaterialTheme.typography.bodyMedium)
                Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_auth_note), style = MaterialTheme.typography.bodyMedium)
                Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_choose), style = MaterialTheme.typography.titleSmall)
                for (c in YoloUi.Choice.entries) {
                    Row(
                        Modifier.fillMaxWidth().selectable(selected = c == choice, role = Role.RadioButton, onClick = { picked = c.name }).minimumInteractiveComponentSize(),
                        verticalAlignment = Alignment.CenterVertically,
                    ) {
                        RadioButton(selected = c == choice, onClick = null)
                        Text(stringResource(c.label), Modifier.padding(start = 12.dp))
                    }
                }
            }
        },
        confirmButton = {
            TextButton(
                enabled = armed,
                onClick = {
                    // Everything is decided again with the clock and the lock of this moment.
                    if (choice != null && YoloUi.mayConfirm(choice, shownAt, elapsed(), isLocked(), connected)) onConfirm(choice)
                },
            ) { Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_confirm)) }
        },
```

```kotlin
package tech.hybriq.adbuddy.yolo

import android.content.Intent

/**
 * The last step to turn YOLO on (spec 2026-10-18, "se confirma con el desbloqueo del móvil"; ruling R22): strong
 * biometrics if the phone has them (BiometricPrompt with BIOMETRIC_STRONG only: the shell can neither inject nor deduce
 * it), else the lock screen credential (KeyguardManager.createConfirmDeviceCredentialIntent: PIN, pattern or password;
 * an agent that watches the key events could learn a PIN: the accepted risk), and with neither (no secure lock) a
 * specific warning, armed, that the person accepts (R20: their decision; the injected tap could then finish the activation).
 * An injected tap cannot pass the first two: the agent could otherwise leave a process running that opens ADBuddy and presses
 * "until the session ends" after the mode expired. The dialog's own checks (YoloUi.mayConfirm) come before; only an OK
 * sends the request.
 */
class YoloAuth(
    /** BiometricManager.canAuthenticate(BIOMETRIC_STRONG) == BIOMETRIC_SUCCESS, and an activity that can show the prompt. */
    private val strongBiometric: () -> Boolean,
    /** KeyguardManager.createConfirmDeviceCredentialIntent: null when the phone has no secure lock. */
    private val credentialIntent: () -> Intent?,
    private val send: (YoloUi.Choice) -> Unit,
    /** No secure lock at all: shows the warning (YoloNoLockDialog); its armed button calls [onResult] true, Cancel false. */
    private val noLock: () -> Unit,
) {
    private var waiting: YoloUi.Choice? = null

    /**
     * Starts the authentication of [choice]: [promptBiometric] shows the biometric prompt (its result comes to
     * [onResult]), or [launchCredential] starts the system's credential screen (its result comes to [onResult] too), or
     * [noLock] shows the warning of a phone with no secure lock (its answer comes to [onResult] as well).
     */
    fun start(choice: YoloUi.Choice, promptBiometric: () -> Unit, launchCredential: (Intent) -> Unit) {
        waiting = null
        if (strongBiometric()) {
            waiting = choice
            promptBiometric()
            return
        }
        val intent = credentialIntent()
        waiting = choice
        if (intent == null) {
            noLock()
            return
        }
        launchCredential(intent)
    }

    /** The authentication ended: ok is a success. A result counts once, and only after a start. */
    fun onResult(ok: Boolean) {
        val choice = waiting
        waiting = null
        if (ok && choice != null) send(choice)
    }
}
```

`android/app/src/main/kotlin/tech/hybriq/adbuddy/yolo/YoloBiometric.kt`:

```kotlin
package tech.hybriq.adbuddy.yolo

import android.content.Context
import androidx.biometric.BiometricManager
import androidx.biometric.BiometricPrompt
import androidx.core.content.ContextCompat
import androidx.fragment.app.FragmentActivity

/** The biometric half of YoloAuth (task A2): thin, with no logic of its own, so it is validated on the emulator (T9) and by hand. */
object YoloBiometric {
    /** Whether the phone has strong biometrics set up (fingerprint or face of the strong class). */
    fun strongAvailable(context: Context): Boolean =
        BiometricManager.from(context).canAuthenticate(BiometricManager.Authenticators.BIOMETRIC_STRONG) == BiometricManager.BIOMETRIC_SUCCESS

    /**
     * Shows the prompt with BIOMETRIC_STRONG only (so no device credential: the negative button cancels) and calls
     * [onResult] once: true on success, false on an error, a cancel or a lock-out. A read that does not match
     * (onAuthenticationFailed) leaves the prompt up and is not a result.
     */
    fun prompt(activity: FragmentActivity, title: String, subtitle: String, cancel: String, onResult: (Boolean) -> Unit) {
        val prompt = BiometricPrompt(
            activity,
            ContextCompat.getMainExecutor(activity),
            object : BiometricPrompt.AuthenticationCallback() {
                override fun onAuthenticationSucceeded(result: BiometricPrompt.AuthenticationResult) = onResult(true)
                override fun onAuthenticationError(errorCode: Int, errString: CharSequence) = onResult(false)
            },
        )
        val info = BiometricPrompt.PromptInfo.Builder()
            .setTitle(title)
            .setSubtitle(subtitle)
            .setAllowedAuthenticators(BiometricManager.Authenticators.BIOMETRIC_STRONG)
            .setNegativeButtonText(cancel)
            .setConfirmationRequired(true)
            .build()
        prompt.authenticate(info)
    }
}
```

```kotlin
/**
 * The last step of the activation on a phone with no secure lock (ruling R20, the person's decision): it says that
 * nothing proves it is them, and that an agent already working on the phone could finish the activation by tapping, even
 * after YOLO expired; the button waits ConfirmUi.ARMING_MS from when it is shown (again each time the app comes back to
 * the front), as Allow does. [onConfirm] is called only by an armed tap; [onDismiss] by Cancel or by leaving the dialog.
 * [elapsed] is the phone's elapsed time (a parameter for the tests).
 */
@Composable
fun YoloNoLockDialog(onConfirm: () -> Unit, onDismiss: () -> Unit, elapsed: () -> Long = SystemClock::elapsedRealtime) {
    var shownAt by remember { mutableLongStateOf(elapsed()) }
    var tick by remember { mutableIntStateOf(0) }
    LifecycleResumeEffect(Unit) {
        shownAt = elapsed()
        onPauseOrDispose {}
    }
    LaunchedEffect(shownAt) {
        delay(ConfirmUi.ARMING_MS + 50)
        tick++
    }
    val armed = ConfirmUi.armed(shownAt, elapsed() + tick * 0L)
    AlertDialog(
        onDismissRequest = onDismiss,
        title = { Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_nolock_title)) },
        text = {
            ObscuredTouchGuard()
            Column(Modifier.verticalScroll(rememberScrollState())) { Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_nolock_body)) }
        },
        confirmButton = {
            // Decided again with the clock of this moment, as the activation dialog does.
            TextButton(enabled = armed, onClick = { if (ConfirmUi.armed(shownAt, elapsed())) onConfirm() }) {
                Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_nolock_confirm))
            }
        },
        dismissButton = { TextButton(onClick = onDismiss) { Text(stringResource(R.string.action_cancel)) } },
    )
}
```

```kotlin
fun YoloBand(view: YoloUi.View, onOff: () -> Unit, modifier: Modifier = Modifier) {
    if (!view.active) return
    val scheme = MaterialTheme.colorScheme
    val spoken = stringResource(R.string.yolo_band_spoken)
    Surface(color = scheme.error, contentColor = scheme.onError, modifier = modifier.fillMaxWidth().testTag(YOLO_BAND_TAG)) {

            verticalAlignment = Alignment.CenterVertically,
        ) {
            Icon(Icons.Default.Warning, contentDescription = null, modifier = Modifier.size(18.dp))
            Text(
                if (view.kind == Kind.SESSION) stringResource(R.string.yolo_band_session) else stringResource(R.string.yolo_band_timed, YoloUi.countdown(view.msLeft)),
                modifier = Modifier.weight(1f).padding(start = 8.dp).semantics { contentDescription = spoken },
                style = MaterialTheme.typography.labelLarge,
            )
            TextButton(onClick = onOff, colors = ButtonDefaults.textButtonColors(contentColor = scheme.onError)) { Text(stringResource(R.string.yolo_band_off)) }
        }
    }
}
```

```kotlin
package tech.hybriq.adbuddy.yolo

import android.app.Notification
import android.app.NotificationManager
import android.app.PendingIntent
import android.content.Context
import android.content.Intent
import androidx.core.app.NotificationCompat
import java.time.format.FormatStyle
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.AdbuddyApp
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.MainActivity
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.R
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.bridge.BridgeState
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.confirm.HostClock
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.ui.Formats

/**
 * The persistent notification of the YOLO mode (spec 2026-10-18): up while it is on, with the link up or down (the mode
 * lives on the host), local to this phone, visible on the lock screen, with one action, Turn off, that needs no unlock
 * (turning it off is always safe). Nothing of the agent's goes in it.
 */
object YoloNotifier {
    const val NOTIFICATION_ID = 8

    /** The key of what is up (YoloUi.notificationKey), null when nothing is. */
    private var shown: String? = null

    @Synchronized
    fun sync(context: Context, state: BridgeState, clock: HostClock) {
        val nm = context.getSystemService(NotificationManager::class.java)
        val key = YoloUi.notificationKey(state)
        if (key == shown) return
        shown = key
        if (key == null) nm.cancel(NOTIFICATION_ID) else nm.notify(NOTIFICATION_ID, build(context, state, clock, offFailed = false))
    }

    /** The Turn off could not be sent: the same notification, saying so. The next change of the mode puts the normal one back. */
    @Synchronized
    fun offFailed(context: Context, state: BridgeState, clock: HostClock) {
        if (shown == null) return
        context.getSystemService(NotificationManager::class.java).notify(NOTIFICATION_ID, build(context, state, clock, offFailed = true))
    }

    /** Takes down what an earlier process left: nobody can say if the mode is still on until the host does. */
    @Synchronized
    fun clear(context: Context) {
        context.getSystemService(NotificationManager::class.java).cancel(NOTIFICATION_ID)
        shown = null
    }

    private fun build(ctx: Context, state: BridgeState, clock: HostClock, offFailed: Boolean): Notification {
        val y = state.yolo
        val text = when {
            offFailed -> ctx.getString(R.string.yolo_off_failed)
            y == null || y.session -> ctx.getString(R.string.yolo_notif_session)
            else -> ctx.getString(R.string.yolo_notif_until, Formats.time(clock.toLocal(y.until), FormatStyle.SHORT))
        }
        val off = PendingIntent.getBroadcast(
            ctx, 8, Intent(ctx, YoloReceiver::class.java).setAction(YoloReceiver.ACTION_OFF), PendingIntent.FLAG_IMMUTABLE or PendingIntent.FLAG_UPDATE_CURRENT,
        )
        val open = PendingIntent.getActivity(ctx, 9, Intent(ctx, MainActivity::class.java), PendingIntent.FLAG_IMMUTABLE)
        return NotificationCompat.Builder(ctx, AdbuddyApp.CHANNEL_YOLO)
            .setSmallIcon(android.R.drawable.ic_dialog_alert)
            .setContentTitle(ctx.getString(R.string.yolo_notif_title))
            .setContentText(text)
            .setCategory(NotificationCompat.CATEGORY_STATUS)
            .setOngoing(true)
            .setOnlyAlertOnce(true)
            .setLocalOnly(true)
            .setShowWhen(false)
            .setVisibility(NotificationCompat.VISIBILITY_PUBLIC)
            .setContentIntent(open)
            // No setAuthenticationRequired: turning it off is safe at any time, locked or not.
            .addAction(NotificationCompat.Action.Builder(0, ctx.getString(R.string.yolo_notif_off), off).setAuthenticationRequired(false).build())
            .build()
    }
}
```

```kotlin
package tech.hybriq.adbuddy.yolo

import android.content.BroadcastReceiver
import android.content.Context
import android.content.Intent
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.AdbuddyApp
import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.bridge.Protocol

/**
 * Receives the Turn off of the YOLO notification (not exported: only this app's PendingIntent reaches it). Turning the
 * mode off is always safe, so it asks nothing, waits for nothing and needs no unlock. If the request cannot be sent (no
 * connection) the notification says so and names `adbuddy yolo off`, which also works from the PC.
 */
class YoloReceiver : BroadcastReceiver() {
    override fun onReceive(context: Context, intent: Intent) {
        if (intent.action != ACTION_OFF) return
        val app = context.applicationContext as AdbuddyApp
        val sent = app.bridge.request(Protocol.YOLO_SET, YoloUi.offRequest()) { } != null
        if (!sent) YoloNotifier.offFailed(app, app.bridge.state.value, app.clock)
    }

    companion object {
        const val ACTION_OFF = "tech.hybriq.adbuddy.action.YOLO_OFF"
    }
}
```

```kotlin
package tech.hybriq.adbuddy.a11y

import tech.hybriq.adbuddy.bridge.BridgeState

/**
 * What the YOLO mode changes in what the helpers protect (spec 2026-10-18, ruling R1 of the plan): the user's protected
 * apps and the heads-up notification block (a 2FA code) stop applying; ADBuddy itself is always protected, or the agent
 * could reach the switch that turns the mode on. Nothing here is a way to turn the mode on: it only reads its state.
 */
object YoloProtection {
    /**
     * Whether the helpers behave as under YOLO: the host said it is on, and the link is up. With the link down, or the
     * state not known, the state may be stale (the host may have turned it off): everything protects as before.
     */
    fun yoloOn(state: BridgeState): Boolean = state.connection == BridgeState.Connection.CONNECTED && state.yolo?.active == true

    /** The predicate UiExecutor gets for "is this package protected": ADBuddy always, the user's list unless YOLO is on. */
    fun effective(yoloActive: () -> Boolean, userProtected: (String) -> Boolean): (String) -> Boolean =
        { pkg -> pkg == Packages.ADBUDDY || !yoloActive() && userProtected(pkg) }

    /** Whether a notification on screen blocks the helpers: not under YOLO. */
    fun notificationBlocks(yoloActive: () -> Boolean, onScreen: () -> Boolean): () -> Boolean =
        { !yoloActive() && onScreen() }
}
```

```go
	e.waitNode(t, "the YOLO band", 20*time.Second, func(n uiNode) bool { return strings.HasPrefix(n.Text, yoloTxtBand) })

	// The notification: ongoing, local to the phone, with Turn off.
	var rec string
	if !poll(20*time.Second, func() bool {
		rec = yoloNotification(e.shell(t, "dumpsys notification --noredact"))
		return rec != ""
	}) {
		t.Fatal("no YOLO notification 20 s after the host said it is on")
	}
	if !slices.Contains(recordActions(rec), yoloTxtTurnOff) {
		t.Errorf("the notification has no %q: %v", yoloTxtTurnOff, recordActions(rec))
	}
	if flags := recordFlags(rec); flags < 0 || flags&flagLocalOnly == 0 || flags&0x2 == 0 {
		t.Errorf("flags %#x: want local-only and ongoing", flags)
	}

	// Off from the notification: one tap, no unlock, no question.
	e.tapInShade(t, "YOLO mode is on", yoloTxtTurnOff)
	off := l.waitFor(t, bridge.TypeYoloSet, 20*time.Second)
	if set := payload[bridge.YoloSet](t, off); set != (bridge.YoloSet{Off: true}) {
		t.Fatalf("the notification asked for %+v, want off", set)
	}
	l.publishRID(t, off, bridge.TypeYoloState, bridge.YoloState{})

	// The band and the notification go when the host says it is off.
	if !poll(20*time.Second, func() bool {
		for _, n := range e.screen(t) {
			if strings.HasPrefix(n.Text, yoloTxtBand) {
				return false
			}
		}
		return true
	}) {
		t.Error("the band is still on the screen 20 s after the host said it is off")
	}
	if !poll(20*time.Second, func() bool { return yoloNotification(e.shell(t, "dumpsys notification --noredact")) == "" }) {
		t.Error("the YOLO notification is still up 20 s after the host said it is off")
	}
}

// The mode outlives the link: with the host gone the band stays, as the mode
```

## Validaciones manuales pendientes

Nada de esto toca el móvil de la persona usuaria sin su permiso explícito. Lo que no se puede automatizar:

1. **Apps protegidas con el servicio de accesibilidad real** (R1 de la app): con YOLO activo, `adbuddy screen` y `adbuddy tap` en una app protegida (en un emulador con el servicio activado, `GuardOnEmulatorTest` es la plantilla) funcionan; con ADBuddy delante siguen bloqueados; al apagar YOLO vuelven a bloquear. Android 17 (Pixel) queda para la persona usuaria.
2. **Notificación de YOLO en la pantalla de bloqueo del Pixel**: visible, con «Apagar», y el botón apaga sin pedir el PIN (Android 12+ con `setAuthenticationRequired(false)`; el entregado con el teléfono bloqueado).
3. **Pausa → apagado desde el móvil real**: pausar con YOLO activo apaga la banda y la notificación a la vez.
4. **Claude Code real**: una pregunta de permiso (`PermissionRequest`) con YOLO se aprueba sin tarjeta y el agente continúa; `AskUserQuestion`/`ExitPlanMode` (que también pasan por ese hook en algunas versiones) no quedan en un estado raro al responderse `allow` sin respuestas. Si ocurre, abrir un pendiente: la spec dice «absolutamente todo».
5. **Caducidad con el PC dormido**: activar 15 min, suspender el PC más de 15 min, despertar: el modo está apagado (se comprueba por el reloj de pared), la app recibe el `yolo.state` apagado.
6. **Cuenta atrás en la banda con un reloj del móvil desajustado**: usa el reloj del host (`HostClock`); comprobar que no salta con otro huso/hora.
7. **Aviso de activación con letra grande y TalkBack**: la lista se lee entera, el botón no se activa por accidente (espera de armado), la banda no lee los segundos.
8. **Que el agente real no consigue activar YOLO tocando la app**: con ADBuddy delante, `adb shell input tap` sobre el interruptor está bloqueado por la guarda (ya cubierto por `TestYoloKeepsADBuddyProtected` y `GuardOnEmulatorTest`); repetirlo en el Pixel con el desbloqueo real.
9. **La autenticación de la activación (A2)** en el Pixel: con huella o cara fuerte sale el `BiometricPrompt` (solo biometría, sin PIN) y cancelarlo no manda nada; en un móvil sin biometría fuerte sale la pantalla del PIN; y un teléfono sin bloqueo de pantalla muestra el aviso específico y, tras el armado, se activa (R20). `YoloBiometric` y el cambio a `FragmentActivity` solo se prueban aquí en el Pixel o en un emulador con huella (`adb emu finger touch`); T9 cubre la rama del PIN.
10. **Un proceso en segundo plano del agente tras la caducidad** (`nohup sh x.sh &` que toca la pantalla): con ADBuddy delante lo bloquea la guarda mientras YOLO esté apagado, y para reactivarlo haría falta la credencial; comprobarlo con un script que abra ADBuddy y toque «Activar» cuando el modo ya caducó.
