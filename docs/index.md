# ADBuddy

Chat from your phone with a coding agent on your PC, and let it manage your Android phone over Wi-Fi adb, safely.

- [Set up your phone](setup/): turn on Wireless debugging and pair the phone.
- [Prepara tu móvil (español)](setup/es/): la misma guía en español.
- [Install](install.md): install the host on your PC, or build it from source.
- [Usage](usage.md): start the host, talk to the agent, answer its questions, look back and undo.
- [Safety](safety.md): how ADBuddy ranks commands, what it keeps in the journal and how to cut access.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): error codes and what to do about them.
- [FAQ](faq.md): short answers to common questions.
